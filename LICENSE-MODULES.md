## Why this scope

These module designs are original work, not fan art of someone else's IP —
unlike `versus-chess`'s character sculpts, there's no third-party rights
issue here. The NonCommercial term is a deliberate choice for a different
reason: it doesn't restrict what *I* (the copyright holder) can do with my
own designs — I can still sell prints, offer a paid customizer, or license
them commercially myself, since NC only binds people who received the file
under this license, not the original creator. What it does do is stop
someone who downloads the free file from MakerWorld/GitHub from reselling it
commercially without a separate agreement, which keeps that option open
rather than giving it away for free on day one.

Module 6 (`handle_dock.scad`) is connector-compatible geometry designed here,
but it docks a third-party sourced model ("The Chuck V2" — see
`docs/sources.md`) that is **not** covered by this license and is not
bundled into this repo.
